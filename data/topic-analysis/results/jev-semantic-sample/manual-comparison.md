# Text-based review of Jev versus original topics

Read 28 selected full segments across all three pilot talks, emphasizing disagreements,
unassigned decisions and a few agreements. See manual-comparison.csv for exact keys,
times, labels and individual judgments. No audio was checked and no predictions
were changed. This is a selective qualitative review by Codex, not an independent
human evaluation or an estimate of corpus-wide accuracy.

Jev resolves obvious unrelated labels and recovers several useful topics from
cluster noise. It also abstains on content where a listed topic would be useful.
Some segments mix subjects, and some cluster names are narrower than their members.
Therefore disagreement is not automatically an error by either method.

Keep both assignments visible and prioritize disagreements for human review.
Separate procedural content, unclear transcript content and substantive content
without a fitting label instead of interpreting every -1 as the same condition.
Use independent per-topic presence questions if multiple topics per segment are
needed; the current Choice probabilities are mutually exclusive primary-topic
alternatives, not presence probabilities.

## Second batch

The additional 14 segments confirm useful recovery from cluster noise and unrelated
labels, but also show defensible original choices. A passage about AI-assisted
writing and solo business supports both 199 and 234. A largely procedural panelist
introduction has no clear primary topic. A closing AI-optimism passage splits
probability equally between 186 and 234 (38% each), making a single returned label
misleading if displayed without alternatives.

Military labels expose vocabulary limitations: personal development in conscription
is not specifically gender-neutral reserve filling; professional military recruitment
and retention is not mainly household crisis preparedness. Topic names and mixed
segment boundaries should be reviewed alongside assignment disagreements.

No accuracy percentage is inferred from these selectively chosen examples.
